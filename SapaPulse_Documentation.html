SAPAPULSE AI

Campus Financial Intelligence, Survival Matrix & Expense Allocator

Document Version v7.0 (Production Release)
Target Demographic Nigerian Tertiary Students
Architecture Client-Side Single File Web Engine
Storage Layer HTML5 Browser LocalStorage

1. Executive Summary & Problem Space

Nigerian tertiary institution students face distinct economic pressures that standard personal finance tools fail to address:

  • Volatile Staple Inflation: Daily fluctuations in the price of foodstuff (Garri, Cooking Gas, Rice, Eggs) render monthly static budgets inaccurate.
  • Surprise Off-Campus Overheads: Irregular compulsory utility dues such as generator fuel levies, NEPA prepaid unit pooling, and water pumping maintenance.
  • The "Mama-Put" Dilemma: Students overspend by relying on canteen meals due to lack of planning, despite bulk cooking offering savings upwards of 35%.
  • Premature Allowance Depletion: Absence of real-time daily spend ceilings leads to sudden mid-semester financial insolvency (commonly referred to as "Sapa").
Project Vision: SapaPulse AI provides a localized, zero-latency, zero-backend financial engine that computes safe daily burn limits, simulates food prep savings, manages hostel bill pooling, and tracks inflation metrics locally.

3. Mathematical Models & Algorithms

SapaPulse AI executes real-time updates across multiple financial indexes:

3.1 Net Safe Balance & Safe Daily Limit

Net_Balance = (Total_Allowance - Fixed_Hostel_Dues) - Total_Logged_Expenses Safe_Daily_Limit = Net_Balance / Remaining_Days

3.2 Garri Emergency Runway Index

Computes survival runway days if non-essential spending is frozen, benchmarked against standard emergency staple sustenance (₦1,000/day):

Runway_Days = Floor(Net_Balance / 1000)

3.3 Cook vs. Buy Comparative Forecasting

Total_MamaPut_Cost = Daily_Canteen_Spend × Forecast_Days Pots_Required = Ceiling(Forecast_Days / Pot_Duration_Days) Total_Cooking_Cost = Pots_Required × Cost_Per_Pot Net_Savings = Total_MamaPut_Cost - Total_Cooking_Cost

4. Functional Modules Breakdown

4.1 Budget Space (Core Ledger & Matrix)

  • Allowance Calibration: Sets total funds, duration, and reserve dues.
  • Instant Spend Deductor: Logs transactions with immediate re-indexing across 7 campus categories.
  • Transaction Ledger: Searchable, filterable list with CSV data export and individual item deletion.

4.2 Ask SapaAI (Context-Aware Advisor)

  • Reads live telemetry (Burn Rate %, Days Remaining, Safe Daily Cap) to produce recommendations:
    • Critical Level (>70% burned): Imposes immediate canteen spending freeze.
    • Food Strategy: Guides bulk soup pot preparations and municipal wholesale markets.
    • Priority Cost Cutters: Recommends midnight data bundles and PDF course-pack pooling.

4.3 Hostel Pool & Utility Splitter

  • Divides communal expenses (e.g., generator fuel, NEPA units) among roommates.
  • Generates formatted receipts with automatic dispatching to WhatsApp group chats.

4.4 Campus Market Inflation Radar

  • Provides regional price benchmarks (Garri, Cooking Gas, Eggs).
  • Includes a crowdsourcing logging mechanism to store local campus market rates.

4.5 Emergency S.O.S Modal

  • Generates a transparent financial deficit breakdown to copy or send directly to guardians via WhatsApp.

5. Step-by-Step User Operational Guide

  1. Step 1: Access the Application: Launch index.html in any modern browser. Click Launch Budget Calculator on the landing page.
  2. Step 2: Calibrate Initial Budget: Under Setup Allowance in Tab 1, enter your Total Pocket Money (e.g., ₦60,000), Days (e.g., 30), and Reserve Dues (e.g., ₦6,000). Click Calibrate Budget Matrix.
  3. Step 3: Log Daily Expenses: As purchases occur (e.g., Handouts, Canteen Meals), enter the description, amount, and category in the Deduct Instant Spend form and submit.
  4. Step 4: Resolve Communal Bills: Switch to the Hostel Pool tab, input the bill details and roommate names, then click Share Receipt to WhatsApp Group.
  5. Step 5: Exporting & Resetting: Export your transaction history at any time using the Export CSV button, or click Reset in the navigation header to clear all stored browser data.

6. Privacy & Storage Specifications

SapaPulse AI adheres to a strict client-side data privacy policy:

  • Zero Remote Servers: Financial figures, personal names, and ledger histories remain exclusively in the user's browser.
  • Storage Namespaces:
    • sp_v7_budget — Stores allowance, planned days, and dues reserve.
    • sp_v7_ledger — Stores historical transaction records.
    • sp_v7_3d_state — Retains user preference for 3D graphics.
    • sp_v7_radar_data — Caches local market benchmark prices.
  • Data Portability: Users retain full ownership with unencrypted CSV export and instant data purge options.
